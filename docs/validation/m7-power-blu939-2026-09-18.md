# M7 seven-profile BLU939 power characterization — 2026-09-18

The LM20B DK completed the seven normalized M7 electrical profiles through one
autonomous `blu939-suite` invocation. The workflow used a configured 3.0 V supply,
nominal 100 ksample/s acquisition, guarded ordinary-RRAM programming, automatic
post-capture RAM checks, and double-read backup plus readback-verified restoration
for both the LM20 and the L15 retry peer. Its local report ID is
`20260918-024732-blu939-suite-kwszvplk`.

The full-window increments over System ON idle were 144.588, 108.221 and
92.143 µA for direct 1/2/4 Mbit/s respectively. The one-second retry profile was
299.075 µA above idle. The bounded Timeslot profile was 76.078 µA above the
BLE-only profile. Different profile durations and finite workload windows are part
of the contract; these values must not be compared as if every workload had the
same duty cycle.

Post-capture observations proved that every direct image completed exactly 1000
scheduled packets. Retry completed 64/64 packets with nine retries, 73 grants and
zero client drops. The L15 peer independently reported eight intentional first-ACK
drops, one post-commit suppression, one valid duplicate and no invalid payload.
The BLE+Timeslot image completed its finite eight-grant/eight-packet burst. The BLE
profiles exercise 100 ms connectable advertising; they do not measure an established
BLE connection or sustained ACL traffic.

Each capture retains every four-byte sample record and the complete normalized CSV.
The six resistance/offset pairs returned by the instrument were applied. Its separate
`Calibrated` field remained `0`; the conversion path does not use that field, so it
is preserved as metadata rather than interpreted as a calibration verdict. Small
negative near-zero samples were integrated without clipping. Reported voltage is the
configured supply value, not an independent rail measurement, and raw range-switch
peaks are not presented as calibrated peak-current claims.

This closes the repository's seven named electrical profiles as fixture
characterization. System OFF, an established BLE connection with sustained ACL,
and USB suspend/remote-wake are distinct workloads and remain separate validation
boundaries.
